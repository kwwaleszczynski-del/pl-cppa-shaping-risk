Corporate PPA structuring and shaping risk on Polish power prices

Quantitative structuring and risk decomposition for pay-as-produced vs profiled/baseload Corporate PPAs on the Polish power market (TGE Day-Ahead / PSE SDAC regime: 435.0 PLN/MWh mean spot price, 0.64 solar capture factor, 0.94 onshore wind capture factor, 354 hours/year of negative or zero prices). Decomposes the settlement gap into four exact additive terms, quantifies fair strike uplifts under negative-price floor clauses, and optimizes hybrid PV + onshore wind + co-located BESS sizing across 500 block-bootstrap weather-price years. Code and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/pl-cppa-shaping-risk.

All contract structures, strike prices, negative-price clauses, and industrial load profiles are illustrative engineering benchmarks calibrated to Polish market characteristics, not legal or financial advice.
