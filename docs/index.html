Corporate PPA structuring and shaping risk on Polish power prices

What a 100 GWh/yr corporate PPA at a fixed strike of 380 PLN/MWh was worth to a renewable seller in 2025, hour by hour, on public PSE data: the RCE price and the national PV and wind output, scaled to the contract volume. Shaping cost, 24/7 hourly CFE, battery dispatch and negative-price clauses are computed from those hours; P50/P90 come from 500 years resampled week by week from 2025. Code and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/pl-cppa-shaping-risk.

Buyer load schedules, battery size and the dispatch rule are illustrative assumptions. Contract clauses and market rules change and should be checked against current sources. Not legal or investment advice.

1. Shaping cost and 24/7 hourly CFE across PV : wind mixes (2025)

Shaping cost is what the seller of a profiled CfD pays to turn its output into the buyer's schedule: the load bought at spot minus the delivered output sold at spot, per contracted MWh, after battery dispatch and curtailment of surplus at negative prices.

2. Seller revenue across 500 bootstrap years

Each bootstrap year is built from whole weeks of 2025 drawn within three weeks of their season, keeping the real price and output of each week together. The spread reflects week-to-week weather and price variation inside one year, not long-term climate or fuel-price uncertainty.

3. Negative-price clauses in a pay-as-produced CfD

In clause hours the CfD pays nothing and the plant curtails. The uplift is the strike increase that keeps the seller's revenue per MWh of available output at 380 PLN/MWh.
